4. Client Obligations: The Client agrees to provide necessary datasets, API keys, and feedback within 48 hours of requests to avoid project stalling.

6. Intellectual Property: Upon full 100% payment, the Client owns irrevocable rights to the specific custom solution. DSW retains rights to our internal foundational architectures (e.g., DexaGen base code).

7. Termination: If the Client cancels the project after Phase 1 completion but before Phase 2 initiation, the initial 30% deposit is non-refundable as it covers immediate operational resources.
